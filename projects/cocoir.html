The Context & Challenge

Upcycling Philippine Coconut Waste

In the Philippines, millions of fibrous coconut husks are discarded annually as agricultural waste, while local farming communities lack a dedicated digital marketplace to distribute high-value coir products for civil engineering, horticulture, and sustainable home living.

The Purpose & Impact

End-to-End Sustainable E-Commerce

COCOIR connects Filipino coconut coir craftsmen directly with nationwide buyers through a responsive storefront paired with persistent cart management, COD & InstaPay payment verification, refund workflows, and real-time Admin inventory & sales analytics.

Key Capabilities

Core Modules & Features

Customer Storefront

  • 5-Category Catalog: Browse 12+ eco-products across Home & Living, Gardening, Construction, Craft & Utility, and Agriculture.
  • Persistent Shopping Cart: Supports both guest sessions and authenticated users with real-time quantity and stock validation.
  • COD & InstaPay Checkout: Auto-fills saved addresses, supports Delivery or Store Pickup, and accepts InstaPay receipt uploads.
  • Order Tracking & Refunds: Track status stages (To Pay to Completed) and submit refund requests with QR proof.

Admin Portal & CMS

  • Executive KPI Dashboard: Monitors today's sales, monthly revenue, order status distribution, and low-stock alerts.
  • Product & Inventory CRUD: Create, edit, soft-delete, and toggle product availability with image uploads and stock control.
  • Payment Verification: Review uploaded customer InstaPay screenshots, approve/reject payments, and update fulfillment states.
  • Dynamic Storefront CMS: Customize store tagline, hero copy, shipping fees, contact details, and social links live.

Analytics & Accounts

  • Sales & Revenue Reports: Daily revenue breakdown comparing InstaPay vs. COD orders, average order value, and top sellers.
  • Role-Based Authentication: Session-guarded MVC access control separating admin and client permissions.
  • Accounts Directory: Admin user management to create accounts, update roles, and toggle active/deactivated status.
  • Self-Service User Profile: Manage personal details, default shipping address book, custom avatar, and password security.
