The Client & Context

Sanctissimo Rosario Ladies Dormitory

Sanctissimo Rosario Building operates as a multi-floor student and professional dormitory in Manila. Daily tasks previously relied on manual paper logs, informal messages, and physical water meter reads, creating delays, disputes, and lack of accountability.

The Purpose & Impact

Automating Community Operations

DormEase digitizes the entire living experience: calculating proportional water consumption per floor automatically, enabling seamless QR Ph payments, and providing tenants with speech-to-text maintenance requests and a 1-tap emergency panic button.

Key Capabilities

Core Modules & Features

Administrator (Web)

  • Room & Occupancy: Manage room allocations, monitor capacities, and track vacancies.
  • Tenant Database: Silent live profile refresh, phone validation, and password resets.
  • Proportional Water Billing: Enter floor readings; system divides usage per room and logs PayMongo QR Ph / deposit receipts.
  • NLP Customization: Dynamic web GUI to train keywords and set urgency levels (Low to Urgent).
  • Announcements & Archival: Sticky pins, scheduled posts, and configurable data retention intervals.

Front Desk (Web)

  • Visitor Registration: Instant check-in verification for tenant pre-registered guests + walk-in logging.
  • Digital In/Out Logbook: Timestamped logging of tenant entries and exits, replacing paper logs.
  • Emergency Alerts: Instant visual and audible alarms triggered by tenant panic button presses.
  • Staff Duty Tracking: Guard schedules, duty shift handovers, and notification preferences.

Tenant (Mobile App)

  • Live Dashboard: Fast glance at room info, current bill (₱106.70), and pending requests.
  • Water Bill & QR Ph: Itemized floor share with 1-click PayMongo QR Ph checkout (GCash/Maya).
  • Voice & NLP Maintenance: Speak or type room concerns; NLP classifies urgency with photo submission.
  • 1-Tap Panic Button: Central red trigger sends immediate distress signals and room location to desk staff.
  • Visitor Pre-Registration: Submit guest details with past-date validation synced to front desk.
